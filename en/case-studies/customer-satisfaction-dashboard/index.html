Customer Satisfaction Intelligence System

A 17-page Power BI report that turns customer survey results into a single view of loyalty, satisfaction and service quality, with drill-down by customer cluster and channel.

Sector
eCommerce · B2B services
Type
BI report & customer analytics
Related service
Business Intelligence & Dashboards

The challenge

Customer satisfaction surveys produced a lot of answers, but the results were hard to read across periods and customer groups. Management needed to see quickly where satisfaction was moving, why customers were unhappy, and how customer care and the website were perceived.

The solution

Survey data is processed in SAS and modelled in Power BI. The report is organised as a guided path, from engagement to loyalty to service detail:

Survey dataRaw questionnaire responses
SAS processingCleaning, scoring and segmentation
Power BI modelMeasures, trends and period comparisons
Report17 pages with navigation and drill-down

What the solution includes

Response rateSurvey engagement and distribution effectiveness
Recommendation & NPSLikelihood to recommend, NPS, NPL and QR index
SatisfactionOverall satisfaction trends over time
DistinctivenessHow unique the offer feels to customers
RepurchasePropensity to buy again
Customer clustersResults by customer segment
Dissatisfied customersReasons and trends behind complaints
Customer careTimeliness, courtesy and competence
Website & feedbackSite experience and a word cloud of comments

Results

  • One report instead of scattered survey extracts
  • Trends and period comparisons on every key indicator
  • Dissatisfaction traced to specific reasons and customer groups
  • Methodology made transparent with a legend and the full questionnaire
